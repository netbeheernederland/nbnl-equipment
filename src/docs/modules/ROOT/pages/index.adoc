This data product requires the use of
https://modellen.netbeheernederland.nl/object-register[NBNL Object Register]
for instantiation of its datasets.

== Required background

* knowledge of connectivity in the CIM;
* knowledge of identification of individuals in the CIM;
* knowledge of mRID persistence and master data management.
